Explainer Studio

Short animated explainers, made and posted by one creator

Explainer Studio is a personal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps make short, original animated videos that explain how everyday things work.

When the owner approves a finished video, the tool can send it to the owner's own accounts:

  • TikTok: the video is uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator then opens the TikTok app, adds the caption, reviews the draft and decides whether to post it.
  • YouTube: the video is uploaded to the creator's own channel as a Short.

Nothing is posted without the creator's approval. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
