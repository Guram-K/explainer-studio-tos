Explainer Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Explainer Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer and is used only with the owner's own accounts. It collects no data from anyone else, has no servers of its own, and never sells or shares data.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok (with your permission, through TikTok Login Kit): your basic profile (open ID and display name, scope user.info.basic) to show which account is connected, and permission to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts (scope video.upload).
  • YouTube (with your permission, through Google sign-in): permission to upload videos to your channel and to read your channel name and the processing status of your uploads.

The tool does not read your messages, followers, contacts, viewing history or any other account data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in tokens TikTok and Google issue are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. They are used only to upload the videos the owner approves. Nothing is sent to anyone other than TikTok and Google themselves.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data

Press Disconnect in the tool to delete the stored tokens, or remove the app's access in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps) or your Google account (Security → Third-party connections).

Children

The tool is not intended for children and is not used by them.
